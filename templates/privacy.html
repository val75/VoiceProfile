Privacy Policy

ShareGud · Last updated: August 16, 2026

Information we collect

  • Phone number — to sign you in via SMS verification codes.
  • Profile information you provide (name, work experience, availability) and the voice recordings and transcripts used to build your profile.
  • An optional profile photo.

How we use it

We use your information to operate the Service: to authenticate you, build and display your worker profile, and improve the product.

SMS messaging

We do not sell or share your phone number, or any information collected as part of SMS consent, with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected for SMS are used solely to deliver sign-in verification codes.

You opt in to SMS when you check the consent box at sign-in. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Message frequency varies and message/data rates may apply.

Data sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us operate (for example, our SMS provider to deliver codes), and only as needed to provide the Service.

Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When it is no longer needed, or per your request, we delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to request that we stop processing it. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@sharegud.com. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we handle such requests in accordance with those laws.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@sharegud.com.
